# Game Dev Tuition

> Game Dev Tuition offers 1 to 1 online tuition in game design and development, including Unity, Blender 3D modelling, Virtual Reality (Meta All In One SDK), Augmented Reality and mobile games. Sessions take place remotely over Discord and cost £35 per hour. The tutors are Certified Unity Developers with a full DBS check.

Key facts:

- Over 14 years of Unity development experience.
- 8 years of teaching game design to 16–21 year olds at UK colleges and online.
- Suitable for students who want extra support with a games development course, and for beginners starting out.
- Students learn at home on their own gaming PC. Unity and Blender are both free to download.
- Tasks are set after each session to complete between sessions.
- Book as few or as many sessions as you like.
